### The gap between what is armed and what is required

The context is required and the arming is real. The gap is *inside* it, and it
is in three places:

1. **Columns is checked, currently FAILS at 114, and does not block.** It runs on
   every invocation and prints its count, so it cannot be forgotten — but a PR
   that adds a phantom *column* merges today. Arming it now would turn every PR
   on the repo red, so this is a backlog, not an oversight; it is the largest
   live gap in the gate.
2. **PARSE COVERAGE is UNKNOWN for 4 modules and is not armed.** Their referents
   are unchecked. Under this repo's own stated rule — "could not evaluate" is not
   "fine" — an unarmed UNKNOWN is a hole with a number on it.
3. **`schema-prm` is required while seeing 2.4% of `.py` engine output** (#4076).
   Required and nearly blind is the more dangerous combination of the two,
   because the green tick reads as coverage. The lint it runs *does* block at the
   emission gate, which carries 100% of `.py` engine output — so the coverage
   exists, but not in the check whose name is on the branch-protection list.

The narrower gap, worth stating because it is what #4089 was about: branch
protection lists **context names**, not the checks inside them. Everything armed
inside `referent-verify` is enforced; everything not armed inside it is invisible
to protection. That is why arming `tables` needed no protection change — and why
the columns gap cannot be seen from the protection API at all.
