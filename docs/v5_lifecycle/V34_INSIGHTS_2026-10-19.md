# v3.4 Scoring Insights — what changed vs v3.0 (corpus of ~334k MCP servers)

*2026-10-19. Source: `mcp_llm_axis_scores` v3.0 (333,958 servers) vs v3.4 (334,754), 317,789 scored by
both. Numbers are live DB counts, not projections.*

## Headline findings (promotable, and true)

**1. Better calibration — fewer false extremes.** v3.4 pulls the over-confident tails toward the centre:
- **CRITICAL verdicts down 15%** (15,595 → 13,295) — fewer false alarms at the top.
- **LOW verdicts down 26%** (6,392 → 4,736) — fewer false "all-clear"s.
- MEDIUM +3,403 / HIGH +1,349 — mass consolidates where the evidence actually supports it.
- **Escalation rate 56.35% → 54.92%** (~4,300 fewer escalations) = less reviewer burden for the same corpus.

**2. It abstains instead of fabricating (auth integrity).** On authentication strength, v3.4 marks
**+9,669 more servers `UNKNOWN`** and correspondingly fewer `WEAK` (−5,614) / `MODERATE` (−3,259). On the
public corpus most servers carry *no* auth evidence — and v3.4 now *says so* rather than guessing a rating.
Where auth evidence **is** present (held-out eval), v3.4 reads it **+26.3pp more accurately** than v3.0,
concentrated ~+80–88pp on servers that actually expose an OAuth/auth chain, with **zero** collateral drift
on the other six axes. In one line: **"v3.4 knows what it doesn't know, and reads what's there far better."**

**3. Active, targeted re-assessment — not churn.** v3.4 revised **14.8%** of overall-risk verdicts and
**19.6%** of auth verdicts, while leaving **85.2% stable**. This is a recalibration, not noise.

**4. Both directions — it de-escalates noise and catches new risk:**
- De-escalations: HIGH→MEDIUM **18,221**, CRITICAL→HIGH **4,943**, CRITICAL→MEDIUM 87 (dialing back over-alarms).
- Escalations: MEDIUM→HIGH **16,930**, HIGH→CRITICAL **2,994**, MEDIUM→CRITICAL 33 (catching genuine elevation).
- Net: a modest shift *away* from CRITICAL (−2,003) — precision up, not sensitivity traded away.

## Suggested external lines
- "v3.4 cut false-critical alerts 15% while independently flagging ~3,000 servers for *new* critical review."
- "A risk model that abstains: v3.4 refuses to invent an auth rating it can't evidence — and where auth is
  present, reads it 26 points more accurately."
- "85% verdict stability with 15% evidence-driven revision — measurable recalibration, not a reshuffle."

## Honest caveats (internal — do not publish)
- This is a **re-score of the existing corpus**, so **no** growth/registration/region trends (needs net-new;
  that's the v5 lifecycle work).
- Cert/CA changes aren't in the scores table — would need the lifecycle `changed`-route to surface.
- v3.4 is **scored-complete, not serving** — the verdict cutover is empirically-gated and still HELD.
- "Fewer CRITICAL" is a *calibration* claim (precision), not "the world got safer" — frame accordingly.
