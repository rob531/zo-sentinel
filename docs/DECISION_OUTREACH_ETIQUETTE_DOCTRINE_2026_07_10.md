# Outreach & Surface Etiquette — Standing Doctrine

*Chairman-ratified 2026-07-10. This is DOCTRINE, not guidance: it applies by
default to every session, every agent, every factory directive, and every
growth tactic, current and future. It is idempotent — re-reading it must never
change it into something weaker; any amendment requires a new council
(3+FATHER) AND explicit chairman ratification. Tag:
`doctrine_outreach_etiquette_2026_07_10`.*

## The principle

**We never abuse a surface we did not build for signups or publicity.**
MCPRisky's entire product is trust. A single spammy interaction — an
unsolicited PR, a cold-email blast, a gamed listing — costs more brand equity
than any funnel it fills. Distribution comes from being useful and being
found, never from imposing on someone else's channel.

## Binding rules

1. **Badging is opt-in, always.** A risk badge is issued ONLY through the
   claim-your-server flow: the maintainer verifies ownership and embeds the
   snippet themselves. We never open a PR, issue, or commit adding our badge
   to a repository we do not own. No exceptions, no "top-50 seeding."
2. **No unsolicited repository interactions.** No cold PRs, no cold issues,
   no cold discussions on third-party repos for promotional purposes.
   Legitimate technical contributions (a real bug fix, a security disclosure
   through the project's stated channel) are unaffected.
3. **Registry submissions follow the host's rules, one at a time.** Before
   submitting mcprisky-mcp (or anything) to any registry/directory: read the
   listing guidelines and TOS, honor open-source requirements, disclose the
   commercial backing plainly, submit ONCE through the official intake, and
   accept rejection without resubmission games. A per-registry compliance
   checklist is completed and archived before each submission.
4. **One announcement per venue.** Show HN, r/mcp, MCP Discord showcase,
   Product Hunt: we announce as ourselves, transparently, once per venue per
   genuine release. No sockpuppets, no engagement farming, no vote asks.
5. **Right of reply everywhere we name a third party.** Every public surface
   that names a server links its dispute/claim path. Methodology stays public
   ("unknown ≠ zero", INSUFFICIENT over guessing). A maintainer objection is
   handled through the dispute flow, fast and in good faith.
6. **No dark-pattern waitlist mechanics.** Referral and claim incentives are
   fine; scraping contacts, pre-checked boxes, or emailing anyone who did not
   sign up are not. Outbound email goes only to addresses that opted in.
7. **The MCP server touches only our own data.** mcprisky-mcp reads the
   MCPRisky corpus; it never probes, calls, or fingerprints third-party MCP
   servers from a user's client.

## What this supersedes

The 2026-07-10 marketing strategy's "open polite PRs adding badges to the ~50
highest-star servers" tactic is RETRACTED (rule 1). The partner-side inversion
stands and is preferred: offering registries our risk data to display with
attribution is cooperation on THEIR terms, not an imposition.

## Enforcement

- Any directive, recipe, or agent plan that violates these rules is closed on
  sight as a doctrine violation (same handling as saturated-family churn).
- Growth/marketing KL docs must cite this doctrine; conflicts resolve in the
  doctrine's favor.
- FATHER note for future councils: growth pressure is the moment this document
  exists for. When a tactic is clever but imposes on someone else's surface,
  the answer is already written here.
